How to Secure Your Seat & US Invitation Letter

A transparent, two-phase participation pathway for international delegates attending the 2027 Summit and East Coast Tour.

Phase 1

Registration & Summit Payment (Module A)

Secure your seat at the Retreat Center & initiate the US Visa process by paying the upfront Module A Summit Fee. Receive your Official Invitation Letter upon payment verification.

Single Occupancy (Approximate): ~$1,200 USD

Includes conference pass, shared/single retreat center lodging, plenary materials, and meals during the core summit.

Notice: Because American venue facilities and retreat quarters are contracted in advance, Phase 1 fee payments are strictly non-refundable.
Phase 2

Tour Package Payment (Module B)

After successfully securing your US Visa using our official invitation letter, access the Member Dashboard to complete payment for the optional 5-Day East Coast Tour Package.

5-Day East Coast Tour Package: Pricing confirmed upon visa verification

Includes guided ministry visits, historic sites, group ground transport, and select dining across the Eastern US seaboard.

Verification Prerequisite: Module B payment access is guarded inside the Member Dashboard and becomes active only after real visa approval is verified.

Delegate Registration
