About Us

WEMA Global — Together Makes a Difference.

Our Story

WEMA Global seeks to connect people and organizations through service, learning, collaboration and meaningful partnerships.

Our platform brings together individuals, volunteers, project leaders, educational communities, churches, nonprofits and other organizations to explore opportunities and work toward shared goals. WEMA Global is operated by World Episcopal Missionary Alliance Inc., a USA Registered Organization in Sheridan, Wyoming.

Vision & Mission

What guides WEMA Global.

Our Vision

A global community where people, organizations, churches, educators and project leaders work together for lasting, meaningful impact.

Our Mission

To connect people and organizations through service, learning, collaboration and meaningful partnerships.
