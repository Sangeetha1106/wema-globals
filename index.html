Who We Are

WEMA Global seeks to connect people and organizations through service, learning, collaboration and meaningful partnerships. Our platform brings together individuals, volunteers, project leaders, educational communities, churches, nonprofits and other organizations to explore opportunities and work toward shared goals.

How WEMA Global Works

Four simple steps. Public pages are open to everyone; membership is needed only for designated member services.

Step 1 — Explore

Browse public pages and learn about WEMA Global initiatives and opportunities.

Step 2 — Register

Create an account and complete membership registration if you wish to access designated member services.

Step 3 — Connect

Explore relevant initiatives, submit enquiries or applications, and connect with appropriate teams.

Step 4 — Participate

Participate in available opportunities for which you meet the applicable requirements.

Note: Membership does not guarantee project funding, employment, admission, accreditation, ordination or partnership approval.

Projects That Seek to Make a Difference

WEMA Crowdfund is designed to receive project proposals, review submissions and help eligible projects explore connections with potential funders.

Stage 1

Submit a proposal

Project leaders share their idea and goals.

Stage 2

Provide requested documents

Supporting documents are requested for review.

Stage 3

Review and verification

The WEMA team reviews the submission.

Stage 4

Approval decision

Each submission receives a decision.

Stage 5

Publication where approved

Approved projects may be published.

Stage 6

Potential funder enquiry

Interested funders may send an enquiry.

Notice: Submission does not guarantee approval or funding.
