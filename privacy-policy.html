Privacy Policy

Introduction

WORLD EPISCOPAL MISSIONARY ALLIANCE INC., publicly known as WEMA Global, respects the privacy of individuals who visit its website, register for membership, submit applications, contact the organization, or participate in its programs.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored, and shared in connection with the website and WEMA Global’s services.

Effective date: to be completed before publication.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the service used, WEMA Global may collect:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Account and membership registration details.
  • Information submitted through application and contact forms.
  • Organization and project information.
  • Event registration and participation information.
  • Information relevant to program eligibility and selection, where applicable.
  • Payment confirmation and transaction-related information provided through the applicable payment process.
  • Technical information relating to website usage, security, and functionality.

Only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose should be collected.

How We Use Information

We use it to run your account, review applications, send acknowledgements and updates, process payments and answer your messages.

What Is Public

Only fields approved for public display are published in the Directory or Partners pages. Private contact information, supporting documents and internal notes are never published by default.

Membership and Program Selection

WEMA Global may use information provided during registration or through relevant applications to administer membership, assess eligibility for applicable programs, communicate with applicants, and manage program participation.

Membership does not guarantee selection for a beneficiary program, leadership program, or event invitation.

Where selection or invitation processes are used, information relevant to the applicable eligibility and selection criteria may be reviewed by authorized personnel.

Leadership Conference and Event Information

If an individual registers for, applies to, or receives an invitation to a WEMA Global event, relevant information may be used to:

  • Process the application or registration.
  • Communicate event details and requirements.
  • Verify eligibility where applicable.
  • Administer invitations and attendance.
  • Provide relevant updates or changes.
  • Coordinate confirmed event arrangements where applicable.
  • Maintain appropriate administrative and financial records.

Any additional information required for travel, accommodation, or other arrangements should be collected only when necessary and handled appropriately.

Payment Information

Payments may be processed through applicable third-party payment providers.

WEMA Global may receive transaction confirmations and related information needed to administer membership or other paid services.

Payment card information may be handled by the relevant payment provider in accordance with its own procedures and policies. WEMA Global does not store card details and will never ask for them by email.

The payment provider will be identified on the website, with a link to its applicable policies, when online payment is connected.

How Information May Be Shared

Personal information may be shared with authorized personnel and relevant service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, administer membership, process payments, manage applications, provide services, maintain security, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Personal information is not sold or disclosed for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

Data Security and Retention

WEMA Global will take reasonable measures appropriate to the services provided to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable legal, administrative, and recordkeeping requirements.

Cookies and Website Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, preferences, analytics, or other disclosed purposes, depending on the technologies actually implemented. Necessary cookies keep the site working; analytics, marketing and third-party cookies are used only if you allow them in Cookie preferences.

The final Privacy Policy and any separate Cookie Policy must accurately reflect the website’s actual practices.

Individual Requests and Contact

Individuals may contact WEMA Global through its official contact channel (Contact Us) to ask questions about their personal information or make a privacy-related request.

Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the organization’s actual procedures.

Policy Updates

WEMA Global may update this Privacy Policy when its services, website functionality, or applicable requirements change.

The published version will display the appropriate effective date and any required update information.
