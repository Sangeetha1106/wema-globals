Information we collect

We collect the information you give us when you register, submit a project, register for a conference, enroll in a WITS course, contact us or make a payment. This includes your name, email, country, organization and payment status.

How we use it

We use your information to run your account, process registrations and payments, send confirmations and receipts, review projects, make introductions and answer your messages.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe and PayPal. WEMA Global does not store card details.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with payment and service providers that help us run the platform, with introduced parties when you agree, and where the law requires.

Security

We use HTTPS, encrypted passwords and access controls. Private documents are never public.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information at any time. Contact us using the details on the Contact page.

Cookies

Necessary cookies keep the site working. Analytics, marketing and third-party cookies are used only if you allow them. You can change your choice under Cookie preferences.
