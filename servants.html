WEMA Servants: The Global Humanitarian Bridge

The premier networking platform connecting Philanthropists (Funders), Grassroots NGOs (Seekers), and Dedicated Volunteers to create tangible global impact under the Direct Bridge Concept.

Join the WEMA Servants Network
Official Legal Notice: “World Episcopal Missionary Alliance Inc. is a legally registered incorporation. Please note: WEMA Global operates strictly as a secure networking and facilitation platform. We provide the digital infrastructure, global directory, and collaborative bridge to connect causes with potential funders. WEMA Global is not a direct funding guarantor.”

Registration Categories

Three distinct entry pathways designed to connect financial resources, grassroots humanitarian projects, and dedicated personnel.

Free Registration

1. Funder / Philanthropist

Fee: $0 (Free)
Free Registration

2. Volunteer / Global Missionary

Skilled professionals, doctors, teachers, vocational workers, and pastoral mission workers ready to be deployed to grassroots initiatives worldwide.

Fee: $0 (Free)
$199 USD Registration

3. Seeker / NGO / Project

Grassroots non-profits, orphanages, and community development projects seeking funding access, project verification, and global volunteers.

Fee: $199 USD (Administrative Contribution)

Core Focus Areas

Deploying humanitarian resources and field personnel where the need is greatest.

Global Humanitarian Initiatives

Sustainable community support, clean water projects, orphan care, and grassroots education across India, Africa, Sri Lanka, and developing nations.

Medical & Health Relief

Mobile medical clinics, prescription dispatches, maternal health, and clinical field partnerships for underserved rural settlements.

Disaster & Emergency Response

Rapid crisis deployment, emergency shelter packages, food security distribution, and disaster rehabilitation logistics.

Global Voluntourism

Structured cross-cultural volunteer journeys uniting practical humanitarian labor with cultural engagement and spiritual ministry.

Non-Refundable Administrative Contribution Policy

All registration fees for the Seeker / NGO / Humanitarian Project category ($199 USD) are strictly non-refundable and are considered as a final, irrevocable administrative contribution towards the infrastructure, vetting, and operational costs of the WEMA Global facilitation bridge.

Before submitting your registration, you must review and confirm agreement with our full terms. For complete provisions, view our dedicated Cancellation & Refund Policy page.

Legal Advisory: Contractual non-refundable clauses do not eliminate statutory consumer protections or mandatory payment processor dispute rights under applicable local and federal financial regulations. Absolute dispute restriction language remains subject to ongoing legal review.

Automated Lead Recovery & Email Pipeline

System architecture for incomplete registration follow-ups and verified account onboarding.

1
Pending Lead Capture

Incomplete Stripe checkout registrations are captured as pending leads in the database upon form submission.

2
First Abandonment Reminder (2 Hours)

Automated cron job sends an email reminder with a secure checkout completion link 2 hours post-abandonment.

3
Second Abandonment Reminder (24 Hours)

Final follow-up notification dispatched 24 hours later detailing mission support access and non-refundable terms.

4
Verified Activation & Receipt Dispatch

Upon Stripe webhook settlement, the user's account is activated, and formal payment receipt and login credentials are dispatched automatically via SMTP.

Backend Dependency Notice: These automated jobs require a live backend server, database, cron scheduler, and transactional email service. They are not simulated or faked in the browser.
