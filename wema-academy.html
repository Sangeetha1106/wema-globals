Financial Aid

WEMA Global Scholarship Program & Financial Aid

“We believe that financial constraints should never hinder a true calling or a visionary leader. WEMA Academy is proud to offer the WEMA Global Scholarship Program for underprivileged and deserving students. During the admission process, our Board of Council Members will carefully review all scholarship requests based on merit and financial hardship. Approved candidates will receive partial or full financial aid applied directly to their Course/Tuition Fees.”

Important Clarification: All applicants, including scholarship seekers, must pay the upfront $199 USD application & document processing fee. Scholarship awards apply solely to course/tuition fees upon Committee approval.

Two-Step Admission & Enrollment Workflow

A strictly sequenced institutional protocol designed to maintain rigorous academic standards and secure student onboarding.

1
Step 1: Application & Council Review

Payment Verification & Under-Review Notice

Submit your application and complete the mandatory $199 USD Application & Processing Fee via Stripe Checkout. All applicants, including scholarship seekers, must pay the upfront application fee.

Automated Step 1 Email Notice

“Your application and payment have been received. Your profile is currently under review by the WEMA Academic Council.”

Security & Protocol Rule: The WEMA Mobile Learning App download link is strictly withheld at Step 1. No application is auto-approved, and no app access is granted prior to Council evaluation.
2
Step 2: Admin Approval & Onboarding

Official Admission Email & App Dispatch

Following thorough evaluation of academic transcripts, pastoral recommendations, or hardship statements by an authorized administrator:

  • Official Admission Decision & Degree Track
  • Scholarship Award Decision (applied exclusively toward future tuition)
  • Confirmed Course / Tuition Fee Details
  • Official WEMA Mobile Learning App Download Link
  • Secure Account Activation Link (never sending plaintext passwords)
Tuition Separation: Any remaining confirmed tuition fees are billed and processed securely within the WEMA Mobile Learning App after formal admission.
