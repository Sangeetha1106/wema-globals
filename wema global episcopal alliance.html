Why Join the WEMA Ecclesiastical Alliance?

“According to Acts 1:8, we are called to be witnesses to the ends of the earth. WEMA Global provides the structured framework to legitimize, support, and expand your calling.”

1. Global Identity & Credentialing

Receive an internationally recognized WEMA Global ID Card and Official Membership Certificate, establishing your credibility with governments, communities, and international agencies.

2. Ordination & Spiritual Covering

For eligible candidates, WEMA provides a pathway to official global ordination (Bishopric, Reverend, or Pastoral) overseen by our International Apostolic Council.

3. Missionary Facilitation & Sponsorship Bridge

While we do not offer guaranteed monthly salaries, WEMA acts as a facilitation bridge. We connect humanitarian and missionary projects with global sponsors. When funding or material support is secured, it is routed through the network and disbursed to the mission field by Local Country Directors, subject to standard administrative deductions.

Application Fee ($99 USD):

“Joining the alliance requires a one-time application and credentialing fee of $99, ensuring that our network consists of committed and verified leaders.”

Strict Transparency Policy:

“Any sponsorship or funding routed through WEMA Global is subject to standard administrative cost deductions to maintain our global infrastructure before the remaining funds are released to the end beneficiary.”
