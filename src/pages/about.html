About WEMA Global

WEMA Global brings people, ministries, organizations and communities together through service, learning, leadership development, project collaboration and other approved initiatives.

Our Mission

To encourage meaningful collaboration, support service-oriented initiatives, develop leadership and learning opportunities, and connect people and organizations around shared goals.

Our Vision

A connected global community where people and organizations can work together to serve, learn, lead and make a positive difference.

Our Values

  • Service and compassion
  • Integrity and accountability
  • Collaboration and respect
  • Learning and leadership development
  • Responsible stewardship
