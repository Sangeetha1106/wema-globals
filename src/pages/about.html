About WEMA Global

Who We Are

WORLD EPISCOPAL MISSIONARY ALLIANCE INC., publicly known as WEMA Global, is an organization incorporated in Wyoming, United States of America.

WEMA Global aims to connect people and organizations through service opportunities, project initiatives, education, leadership development, and global collaboration.

Our work is organized through dedicated initiatives designed to support participation, learning, responsible project engagement, and meaningful connections.

Our Mission

To connect people and organizations, encourage meaningful service, support purposeful initiatives, and promote leadership and educational opportunities through responsible collaboration.

Our Vision

To build a connected global community that encourages service, learning, leadership, and cooperation for meaningful and sustainable impact.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity — We value honesty, accountability, and responsible conduct.
  • Service — We encourage meaningful participation and service to communities.
  • Collaboration — We believe cooperation can help people and organizations work toward shared goals.
  • Learning — We support opportunities for education, leadership development, and knowledge sharing.
  • Transparency — We aim to communicate our processes, eligibility requirements, and responsibilities clearly.
  • Respect — We value respectful engagement among people and organizations from different backgrounds.

Who Can Participate?

Depending on the relevant initiative and its eligibility requirements, participation may be open to volunteers, members, project applicants, organizations, churches, ministries, educational institutions, and other eligible individuals or entities. Some activities may require registration, membership, application review, or an invitation.

Membership does not automatically guarantee project funding, event invitations, selection as a beneficiary, admission to a program, or approval for any particular service.

Our Commitment to Transparency

WEMA Global aims to communicate its membership requirements, fees, application processes, program conditions, and organizational information clearly.

Participation in a program or submission of a project does not guarantee acceptance, funding, or a particular outcome.
