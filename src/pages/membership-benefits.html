WEMA Global Membership Benefits

Membership is intended to provide access to applicable member services and opportunities across WEMA Global, subject to the requirements and availability of each initiative.

Proposed benefits

Benefits are listed as proposed and are confirmed by each initiative when services are available.

Central member account

One account for your WEMA Global activity.

Designated member services

Access to designated member services and support channels.

Initiative enquiries

Ability to enquire about relevant initiatives.

Programme eligibility

Eligibility to apply for applicable programmes.

Member updates

Member updates where available.

Connections

Opportunities to explore connections with relevant participants and organizations.

Learning & collaboration

Access to relevant learning, service and collaboration information.

Important: Membership does not guarantee funding, grants, employment, admission, accreditation, ordination, partnership approval, investment or any specific outcome.

Become a member

One membership registration fee of USD $199 covers the whole WEMA Global platform.
