Registration of interest

Submitting an event form is not a confirmation of attendance or an invitation.

Invitations

Invitations are subject to eligibility, available capacity and confirmation by WEMA Global. An invitation does not guarantee visa approval or entry into the United States. Participants are responsible for meeting applicable immigration and travel requirements.

Fees and changes

Any applicable conference fee, and cancellation or change terms, are communicated before payment and are separate from membership.
