Membership with WEMA Global is voluntary. Visitors may explore publicly available website information without becoming members. Eligible volunteer opportunities may be available without a membership fee.

Individuals who choose to become members and pay the annual membership fee of USD $199 may access designated membership benefits and member-only application submission facilities, subject to WEMA Global’s applicable policies, eligibility requirements and membership terms.

No individual is required to become a member against their will. Membership is a voluntary choice for individuals who wish to access designated member benefits.

Membership does not guarantee approval of applications, project funding, admission to a program, credentials, certificates or any particular outcome. Membership fee and other program or service fees are separate, and fees are disclosed before payment.
