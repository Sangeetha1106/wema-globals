What is WEMA Global membership?

WEMA Global membership is intended to provide a central point of access to applicable member services, participation pathways and support across the platform.

Public website information remains accessible to everyone. Membership is needed only for designated member services, and any page that needs it will say so clearly.

Membership Registration Fee
USD $199

The overall WEMA Global membership registration fee — not a separate fee for each initiative.

Please note: Existing members will not be charged again for the same valid membership. Validity period and any renewal terms will be published only after they are confirmed by WEMA.

Separate charges, explained up front

Some activities may carry their own charges. They are never mixed with the membership fee.

Conference tickets

If a conference ticket fee applies, it will be shown clearly on the event page once confirmed.

Course tuition

Academy fees, if any, will be published with the course details once confirmed.

Store purchases

Product prices and delivery information are shown with each listing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay to read the website?

No. All public information pages can be read without membership.

Is the $199 fee charged for each initiative?

No. It is one overall membership registration fee for WEMA Global.

Does membership guarantee funding, admission or approval?

No. Membership does not guarantee funding, grants, employment, admission, accreditation, ordination, partnership approval, investment or any specific outcome.

Can I get a refund?

Refund and cancellation terms are set out in the Membership Policy and the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
