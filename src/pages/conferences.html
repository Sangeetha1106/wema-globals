Global Leadership Conference

Register Your Interest

The Global Leadership Conference brings together eligible WEMA Global beneficiaries and invited participants for connection, learning, leadership development and collaboration.

Invitation. Eligible beneficiaries and participants associated with WEMA Global programs may be considered for an invitation, subject to eligibility, available capacity and confirmation by WEMA Global. An invitation does not guarantee visa approval or entry into the United States. Participants are responsible for meeting applicable immigration and travel requirements.

Conference Registration

Please submit the conference registration/application form for review. The WEMA Global team will contact eligible applicants regarding registration status, invitation details and any applicable conference fee.
