Crowdfunding Policy

How project proposals are submitted, reviewed and published.

EligibilitySubmissionReview & verificationApproval & publicationFunder contactFeesHandling of fundsFraud prevention
World Episcopal Missionary Alliance Inc. (WEMA Global)Status: draft outline, final wording subject to WEMA approval and legal review

Eligibility

Project leaders and organizations with a clear proposal may submit it for review.

Submission

Submissions must be accurate and complete. Submission does not guarantee approval or funding.

Review & verification

The WEMA team reviews submissions and may request documents to verify them.

Approval & publication

Each submission receives a decision. Approved projects may be published.

Funder contact

Interested funders may send an enquiry about a published project.

Fees

No separate Crowdfund fee is announced at this time. Any approved fee will be stated here and explained in advance.

Handling of funds

Whether WEMA handles funds directly will be confirmed here once the relevant processes and legal arrangements are in place. Until then, direct collection or distribution of funds is not offered.

Fraud prevention

WEMA Global may refuse, remove or report submissions that appear fraudulent or misleading.
