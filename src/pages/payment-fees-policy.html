Separate fees

The annual membership fee (USD $199), program or service fees, conference fees and store purchases are separate. Any applicable fee is disclosed before payment and is not combined with another payment.

How payments are confirmed

Online payments are confirmed through the payment provider. Bank transfers, if offered, are verified manually. We never ask for card details by email.

Tax status

WEMA Global is a non-profit organization. We are currently in the process of applying for our 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status. Please note that at this time, your membership dues and voluntary contributions are not tax-deductible.

Receipts

A payment confirmation with a payment reference is sent after a payment is confirmed. Payment does not guarantee approval unless explicitly stated in the applicable program terms.
