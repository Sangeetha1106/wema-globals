Fees

WEMA Global may charge fees for membership and for certain separately offered services, where applicable.

The annual membership fee is USD $199, subject to the applicable terms displayed before payment.

Separate fees

Other services, including certain courses, conferences, events, directory services, or store purchases, may have separate fees. Any such fee is clearly identified before the user agrees to pay.

The website discloses the applicable amount, currency, purpose of payment, and relevant conditions before payment is submitted.

Payment is not approval

A payment confirmation does not automatically mean that a separate application has been approved or that an invitation, funding decision, course admission, or service outcome has been granted.

Membership and service activation follow the applicable verification and approval process.

How payments are confirmed

Payments are processed through the actual payment provider integrated with the website. The website does not show a successful payment status unless the payment has been appropriately verified.

Receipts

A payment confirmation with a payment reference is sent after a payment is confirmed.
