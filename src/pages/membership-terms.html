Voluntary membership

WEMA Global Membership is subject to the applicable registration requirements, eligibility criteria, membership period, and published terms. Membership is voluntary. Public information and eligible volunteering remain free, and no individual is required to become a member against their will.

Annual fee

The annual membership fee is USD $199, subject to the applicable membership offer and the terms displayed before payment. It covers the overall platform and is not charged separately for each initiative.

Separate charges

Membership is separate from donations, project funding, conference registration fees, course fees, and other separately charged services.

No guarantee

Membership does not guarantee selection for a leadership program, beneficiary program, event invitation, project funding, course admission, a visa, or any government approval.

Accurate information

Members must provide accurate registration information and use their accounts responsibly.

Activation

Membership activation is subject to completion of the applicable registration and payment verification process. Application status, payment status and membership status are tracked separately.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Renewal, cancellation, and refund conditions are disclosed clearly before payment. Automatic renewal is not activated without appropriate disclosure and user authorization.

Administration

WEMA Global may administer membership in accordance with these Terms and applicable law.
