Voluntary membership

Membership is voluntary. Public information and eligible volunteering remain free. No individual is required to become a member against their will.

Annual fee

The annual membership fee is USD $199. Membership fee and other program or service fees are separate and are disclosed before payment.

Benefits

Members may access designated membership benefits and member-only application submission facilities, subject to WEMA Global’s policies and eligibility requirements. Membership does not guarantee approval of applications, project funding, admission to a program, credentials, certificates or any particular outcome.

Renewal

Auto-renewal is off unless the member or organization separately approves it.

Status

Membership becomes active only after payment is successfully confirmed. Application status, payment status and membership status are tracked separately.
