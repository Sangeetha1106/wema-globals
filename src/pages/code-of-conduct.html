Respectful communication

Treat everyone with respect and courtesy.

Accuracy

Share information that is accurate and not misleading.

Confidentiality

Respect the privacy and confidentiality of other people and organizations.

Fraud and harassment

Fraud, harassment, discrimination and abuse are not tolerated.

Responsible submissions

Submit only content and projects you are authorized to share.

Reporting concerns

Report concerns through the Contact page.
