Code of Conduct

How we expect people to take part in WEMA Global.

Draft outline. The full legal text must be reviewed by WEMA’s legal adviser before it is published as final.

Respect

Treat other participants, staff, volunteers and partners with courtesy and respect.

Honesty

Provide accurate information in every application and do not claim approvals, affiliations, credentials or recognition that you do not hold.

Safety and integrity

Harassment, discrimination, fraud and misuse of WEMA Global names, logos or platforms are not accepted.

Reporting concerns

Concerns can be reported through the Contact Us page. WEMA Global may restrict participation where this Code is not followed.
