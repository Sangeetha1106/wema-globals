Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us, such as your name, email, country, organization and the details you include in forms, enquiries, applications and registrations.

Why we use it

We use information to run your account, respond to enquiries, review applications and submissions, and provide the services you request.

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the platform, with other parties where you ask us to, and where the law requires.

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described, or as required by law.
