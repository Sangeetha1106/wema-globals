Information we collect

We collect the information you give us when you create an account, apply to an initiative, submit a project, apply for a directory listing, register interest in an event, contact us or pay. This can include your name, contact details, country, organization details, uploaded documents and payment status.

How we use it

We use it to run your account, review applications, send acknowledgements and updates, process payments and answer your messages.

What is public

Only fields approved for public display are published in the Directory or Partners pages. Private contact information, supporting documents and internal notes are never published by default.

Payments

Payments are handled by a supported payment provider. WEMA Global does not store card details and will never ask for them by email.

Sharing and security

We do not sell your information. Documents are stored privately and only authorized reviewers can access them. Access is role-based and administrative actions are logged.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or remove your information. Contact us through the Contact Us page.

Cookies

Necessary cookies keep the site working. Analytics, marketing and third-party cookies are used only if you allow them in Cookie preferences.
