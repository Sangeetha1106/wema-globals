WEMA Global Episcopal Alliance

Apply for Alliance Membership

The WEMA Global Episcopal Alliance brings together eligible leaders, churches, ministries and organizations interested in collaboration, fellowship and leadership development. Membership and participation are subject to the Alliance’s applicable eligibility requirements and review process.

Membership and Participation

Eligible applicants may be considered for Alliance membership and the specific activities or services described in the applicable program information. Submission of an application does not guarantee acceptance, credentials, ordination, certification or any particular outcome.
