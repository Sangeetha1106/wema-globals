WEMA Global Honours & Awards

Recognizing Service, Leadership, and Meaningful Contributions

WEMA Global Honours & Awards aims to recognize individuals and organizations whose leadership, service, dedication, and contributions reflect the values and mission of WEMA Global.

Through this initiative, WEMA Global seeks to acknowledge meaningful contributions to leadership development, education, missionary service, community engagement, and organizational collaboration.

Nominations and Selection

Information about nomination procedures, eligibility criteria, supporting documents, selection procedures, and submission deadlines will be announced when the awards program is officially approved.

Submitting a nomination does not guarantee selection for an award. All nominations will be reviewed according to the officially approved criteria and procedures.

Awards Announcement

Details regarding award recipients and presentation ceremonies will be announced through official WEMA Global communication channels after confirmation.
