WEMA Project Fund

Submit a Project

WEMA Project Fund provides a process for organizations and project owners to submit project proposals for review and to learn about potential funding opportunities.

Important. Please be advised that the processing of project funds does not follow a fixed timeline or have a specific deadline.

Project Submission

Submit your project proposal for review by WEMA Global. Provide accurate organization and contact details, project objectives, budget, requested funding, timeline, intended beneficiaries and supporting documents. Submission does not guarantee approval or funding. Our team will review the information and contact you about possible next steps.

Funding Opportunities

Learn about WEMA Global’s project review process, eligibility considerations and the information required when submitting a proposal. Funding decisions depend on review, available resources and applicable policies. Funding is not guaranteed.

Project Submission Disclaimer

Please note. Submitting a project is not a guarantee of funding. WEMA Global does not state that funds will be provided directly unless funding arrangements for a project have been confirmed. Any service or administration fee will be shown separately, with its amount, purpose and terms, before you proceed.
