How this policy works

Different payments follow different rules. Each rule is stated below and is shown again at the time of payment. Charges for one activity are never mixed with another.

Membership

Cancellation and refund terms for the USD $199 membership registration fee will be stated here once approved by WEMA.

Conference tickets

Cancellation and refund terms for any conference ticket will be published with the confirmed event details.

Course tuition

Cancellation and refund terms for any course fee will be published with the confirmed course details.

Store purchases

Returns, cancellations and refunds for store products will be provided with each product listing.

Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any consumer rights you have under applicable law.
