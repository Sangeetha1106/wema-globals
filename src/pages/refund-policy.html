Strictly non-refundable

All payments and fees are strictly non-refundable and are considered as a final donation towards the missions and operations of WEMA Global. This applies to WITS tuition, conferences, membership and donations.

Your agreement

Before every payment you must tick a checkbox to confirm this. You cannot pay without it.

Payment receipts

You receive a confirmation email and a payment receipt automatically after each payment. A receipt is a payment confirmation, not a tax document.

Store purchases

Products from the WEMA Store are bought on Amazon or Shopify. Their return policies apply.

Questions

If you were charged by mistake, contact us through the Contact page with your receipt details.
