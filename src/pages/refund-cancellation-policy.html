Refund and cancellation eligibility

Refund and cancellation eligibility depends on the service purchased and the terms disclosed before payment.

Before completing a payment, users should review the applicable refund, cancellation, and renewal conditions for membership and any separately charged service.

How to request

Requests must be submitted through the official contact channel or the applicable service process. Each request will be reviewed according to the applicable published terms and relevant legal requirements.

Approved refunds

Where a refund is approved, the process and timing will depend on the payment method and the applicable payment provider.

Cancellation of a service does not automatically establish eligibility for a refund unless the applicable terms provide for one.

WEMA Store

Store orders are a separate purchase. Shipping, return and refund terms for store products are stated on each product page and published here before store orders open.
