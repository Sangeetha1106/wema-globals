Programs vary

Course content, admission requirements, fees, certificates and any recognition or accreditation arrangements vary by program. Review the details published for each course before applying.

Educational collaborations

The role of each collaborating institution, awarding institution, certificate terms and recognition must be confirmed before enrollment. No accreditation is claimed unless it has been confirmed for the specific program.
