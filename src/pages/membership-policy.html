Membership fee

The WEMA Global membership registration fee is USD $199. It is one overall fee for the platform, not a separate fee for each initiative. Existing members are not charged again for the same valid membership.

Scope of membership

Membership is intended to give access to applicable member services and participation pathways, subject to the requirements and availability of each initiative.

Public versus member access

Public information pages are open to everyone. Pages or services that require membership will say so clearly.

Activation & validity

Activation timing and the validity period of membership will be stated here once confirmed by WEMA.

Renewal

Renewal terms apply only if and when they are approved and announced by WEMA.

Cancellation & refunds

See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

No guarantees

Membership does not guarantee funding, grants, employment, admission, accreditation, ordination, partnership approval, investment or any specific outcome.
