WEMA Servants

Serve. Connect. Make a difference.

WEMA Servants connects people who want to serve with opportunities to volunteer, support eligible initiatives and submit project requests for review.

Choose how you would like to take part

Volunteer

Share your skills, experience and interests to be considered for eligible volunteer opportunities.

Apply as a Volunteer

Funder

Tell us about your funding interests and the types of initiatives you may wish to support. Submission does not create an obligation to fund any project.

Register Your Interest as a Funder

Seeker / NGO

Organizations and eligible project seekers may submit information about their organization and proposed project for review. Submission does not guarantee approval or funding.

Submit a Project Request
Eligible volunteering is free. WEMA Global does not promise approval or funding for any application. See the Project Submission Disclaimer.
