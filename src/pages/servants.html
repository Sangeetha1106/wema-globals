Connecting people who want to serve

WEMA Servants connects people interested in volunteering, community service and meaningful opportunities to serve others.

Eligibility & requirements

  • Public information is open to everyone.
  • Designated member services require WEMA Global membership.
  • Each opportunity may have its own requirements, which will be stated with the opportunity.

Approved fees

Access to designated member services requires the single WEMA Global membership (USD $199). This is one overall membership fee, not a separate fee for each initiative.

No other fee is shown for this initiative at this time. Any separate charge will be explained clearly and in advance, once it is confirmed by WEMA.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need membership to express interest?

No. You can send an interest form without membership. Some member services may require membership later.

Is funding or a position guaranteed?

No. WEMA Global does not guarantee funding, employment or any specific outcome.
