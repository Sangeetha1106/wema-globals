Tax-Exempt Status Information

WEMA Global is incorporated in Wyoming, United States of America. Incorporation alone does not establish federal tax-exempt status under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

WEMA Global’s federal tax-exempt status must be described accurately based on official IRS documentation. Unless and until applicable tax-exempt recognition is officially confirmed, WEMA Global is not represented as an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) organization, and membership fees or payments are not described as tax-deductible charitable contributions.
