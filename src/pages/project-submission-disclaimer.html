Review only

Submitting a project to WEMA Servants or WEMA Project Fund does not guarantee approval or funding. All submissions are reviewed. WEMA Global does not promise direct funding unless its funding arrangements for a specific project have been confirmed. Any service or administration fee is shown separately, with its amount, purpose and terms, before you proceed.

Funding

Funding decisions depend on review, available resources and applicable policies. Funders are under no obligation to fund any project.

Accuracy

Applicants confirm that the information and documents they submit are accurate. Nothing on this website is financial, legal or tax advice.
