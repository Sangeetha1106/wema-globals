No guarantee

Submitting a project proposal through WEMA Global does not guarantee funding, approval, partnership, or implementation.

Review

All project submissions may be subject to eligibility checks, review, verification, and other applicable procedures.

Accuracy

Applicants are responsible for providing accurate information and any required supporting documents.

Decisions

Any funding decision or related communication will be provided through the applicable official process. Applicants should not assume that a project has been approved or funded unless they receive formal confirmation from WEMA Global.

Timeline

Please be advised that the processing of project funds does not follow a fixed timeline or have a specific deadline.

Funding arrangements

WEMA Global does not state that funds will be provided directly unless funding arrangements for a specific project have been confirmed. Any service or administration fee is shown separately, with its amount, purpose and terms, before you proceed.
