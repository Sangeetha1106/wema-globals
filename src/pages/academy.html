Educational Collaborations

WEMA Academy is developing educational collaborations with institutions including Regency School of Mission and Ministry (Canada), Global University (United States of America), and Miracle Academy (Canada). The role of each institution, course availability, awarding institution, certificate terms, recognition and accreditation arrangements depend on the specific program and must be confirmed before enrollment.

Course disclaimer. Course content, admission requirements, fees, certificates and any recognition or accreditation arrangements vary by program. Please review the details published for each course before applying. See the Academy/Course Terms.
