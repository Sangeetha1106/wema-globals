WEMA Academy

Theological Studies, Secular Studies & Ministry Training

Apply for a Course

WEMA Academy offers learning opportunities in theological studies, secular education, and ministry training through its academic and educational affiliations.

A. Theological Studies

Bachelor of Theology (B.Th.)3 Years
Master of Theology (M.Th.)2 Years
Doctor of Ministry (D.Min.)1 Year
Doctor of Divinity (D.D.)Thesis-based program, 6 Months
Ministry Certification & Pastoral Training1 Year of training under senior pastors

Theological studies and ministry training are intended to support biblical understanding, leadership development, and practical ministry preparation.

Academic Affiliations and Educational Connections

WEMA Academy’s proposed academic affiliations and educational connections include Regency School of Mission and Ministry, Canada; Miracle Academy, Canada; Global University, USA; and Serampore University, India. The specific role of each institution, including course delivery, academic oversight, degree awarding, and recognition, is subject to confirmation.

The Doctor of Divinity (D.D.) program may include thesis-based study and honorary recognition, subject to the applicable program requirements and approval.

B. Secular Studies — Diploma Programs

WEMA Academy also plans to offer diploma-level learning opportunities in the following fields:

  1. International Human Rights and Law
  2. Information Technology (IT)
  3. NGO Management

These programs are intended to support professional knowledge, practical skills, and organizational development.
