WEMA Crowdfund

Connect Projects with Potential Funders

Submit a Project Crowdfunding Policy

Projects That Seek to Make a Difference

WEMA Crowdfund is designed to receive project proposals, review submissions and help eligible projects explore connections with potential funders.

Notice: Submission does not guarantee approval or funding. Direct collection or distribution of funds is not offered unless the relevant processes and legal arrangements have been confirmed and announced.

How it works

From proposal to potential funder enquiry.

Stage 1

Submit a proposal

Share your project idea and goals.

Stage 2

Provide requested documents

Supporting documents are requested during review.

Stage 3

Review and verification

The WEMA team reviews and verifies the submission.

Stage 4

Approval decision

You receive a decision.

Stage 5

Publication where approved

Approved projects may be published.

Stage 6

Potential funder enquiry

Interested funders may enquire.

Eligibility & requirements

  • Project leaders and organizations with a clear project proposal.
  • Requested documents must be accurate and verifiable.
  • Eligibility, verification and any approved fees are described in the Crowdfunding Policy.

Approved fees

Access to designated member services requires the single WEMA Global membership (USD $199). This is one overall membership fee, not a separate fee for each initiative.

No separate Crowdfund submission fee is shown at this time.

No other fee is shown for this initiative at this time. Any separate charge will be explained clearly and in advance, once it is confirmed by WEMA.
