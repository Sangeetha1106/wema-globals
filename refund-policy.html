Non-Refundable & Donation Policy

All payments, base registration fees ($199 USD), conference pass fees, Academy student enrollments, directory subscriptions, and contributions made to WEMA Global are strictly non-refundable and are considered as a final, irrevocable donation towards the missions, humanitarian operations, and digital facilitation infrastructure of WEMA Global.

Mandatory Acceptance Checkbox

Before proceeding with any payment or registration, every user is required to actively accept this policy by checking an unchecked mandatory checkbox with the exact declaration:

“I have read and agree to the Non-Refundable & Donation Policy.”

The checkout and registration workflows are locked and will not execute unless this box is explicitly marked. Note: This policy does not eliminate statutory consumer protections or merchant dispute rights under applicable local and federal financial regulations, and is subject to formal legal review.

Universal $199 Registration & Accreditation Fees

The universal $199 USD base registration fee applies to all core pillars—including WEMA Servants, WEMA Crowdfund, WEMA Academy, WEMAX Global Directory, and WEMA Episcopal Alliance. Access to member dashboards, directory databases, and accreditation certificates remains strictly conditional upon verified payment settlement.

Receipts & Documentation

An automated payment confirmation receipt is generated upon verified transaction completion through our payment processors (Stripe / PayPal). A payment receipt serves as transaction verification for your records and does not constitute a legal or financial guarantee of funding.

Store Purchases

Physical merchandise listed through the WEMA Store (such as Coco Peat, apparel, and clerical vestments) are fulfilled through external third-party platforms (Amazon, Shopify). Returns and exchanges for those items are governed by the respective platform policies.

Legal Disclaimer & Status

Official Platform Disclaimer: “World Episcopal Missionary Alliance Inc. is a legally registered incorporation. Please note: WEMA Global operates strictly as a secure networking and facilitation platform. We provide the digital infrastructure, global directory, and collaborative bridge to connect causes with potential funders. WEMA Global is not a direct funding guarantor.”
