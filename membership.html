WEMA Global Membership

Become Part of a Global Community.

Become a Member Review Membership TermsContact Us

WEMA Global Membership provides an opportunity for eligible individuals to register their interest in the organization’s initiatives and participate in applicable member services, subject to the relevant eligibility requirements and program conditions.

Membership is intended to support engagement with WEMA Global’s initiatives, communications, and eligible participation opportunities. Membership is voluntary, and eligible volunteer opportunities may be available without a membership fee.

Annual Membership Fee

The annual WEMA Global Membership Fee is USD $199.

The applicable fee, membership period, benefits, renewal terms, and conditions will be displayed before payment.

Membership is separate from donations, project funding, conference fees, course fees, and other separately charged services.

The annual membership fee does not guarantee project funding, beneficiary selection, a leadership conference invitation, admission to a course, a visa, or approval for any other service.

Membership fee. The annual WEMA Global membership fee is USD $199 per year for the overall platform. It is not a separate membership fee for each initiative. Any additional course, conference, product or service fee is a separate fee and is clearly identified and disclosed before payment.

Membership Benefits

Depending on the member’s eligibility and the services available, membership may provide:

  • Access to applicable member services.
  • Opportunities to learn about WEMA Global initiatives.
  • Eligibility to apply for programs that require membership.
  • Access to relevant membership communications.
  • Eligibility for consideration for selected programs or invitations, where applicable.

Specific benefits depend on services actually available and the requirements of each program.

How Membership Works

  1. Complete the membership registration form.
  2. Review the applicable membership terms and fee.
  3. Complete payment if membership requires payment.
  4. Receive confirmation after the payment has been successfully verified.
  5. Access the member services for which you are eligible.

Membership approval and activation follow the published eligibility requirements and the actual registration and payment process.

Membership and Leadership Conference Selection

WEMA Global may identify eligible members or other eligible individuals for consideration for selected leadership programs and events. Selected individuals may receive an official invitation based on the applicable selection process.

Membership does not automatically entitle a person to receive an invitation or to be selected as a beneficiary.

Leadership Program Selection and Invitations

WEMA Global may identify and select eligible individuals for consideration for specific leadership programs and events.

Selection may take into account the purpose of the program, eligibility requirements, relevant experience, participation criteria, and the needs of the event.

Selected individuals may receive an official invitation with details about the program, registration process, participation requirements, deadlines, and any applicable costs or arrangements.

Membership alone does not guarantee beneficiary selection or an invitation. An invitation must not be treated as confirmation of travel arrangements, visa approval, immigration status, or any other government authorization.

Membership Fees and Renewal

The annual membership fee is USD $199. Any renewal terms, cancellation procedures, refund eligibility, and applicable deadlines are disclosed clearly before payment. Automatic renewal or recurring charges are not enabled without the required clear disclosure and user authorization. See the Membership Terms and Refund and Cancellation Policy.
