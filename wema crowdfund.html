How WEMA Crowdfund Works

A transparent, three-step bridge connecting visionary entrepreneurs and humanitarian pioneers with worldwide capital.

Step 1

1. Create & Validate

Register your profile, submit your compliance KYC documents, upload your comprehensive pitch deck or humanitarian proposal, and establish your clear funding target amount.

Step 2

2. Showcase to the World

Campaigns are separated into two dedicated wings: the Non-Profit Wing (charities, disaster relief, social healthcare, orphan care) and the For-Profit Wing (technology startups, green ventures, commercial expansion).

Step 3

3. Connect & Fund

Accredited international investors, institutional funds, and philanthropists browse verified campaigns and connect directly with campaign creators to deploy capital.

Automated Lead Recovery & Reminder Pipeline

How incomplete Stripe registrations and abandoned checkouts are preserved and re-engaged.

1
Pending Lead Capture & Database State

When a campaigner submits the form, registration details and pending checkout state are saved securely in the backend database prior to redirecting to Stripe.

2
First Abandonment Reminder (2 Hours)

If checkout remains uncompleted, an automated server cron job dispatches the first email reminder with a one-click session link 2 hours post-submission.

3
Second Abandonment Reminder (24 Hours)

A second follow-up notification is dispatched 24 hours later detailing campaign visibility, annual renewal policies, and the non-refundable terms.

4
Verified Account Activation via Stripe Webhook

Campaigner accounts are activated exclusively when a verified server-to-server Stripe webhook confirms payment settlement. No account is ever unlocked via browser parameters or success URLs.

Backend Integration Requirement: Automated lead recovery and reminder dispatches require backend database persistence, cron scheduling workers, and transactional SMTP configuration.
